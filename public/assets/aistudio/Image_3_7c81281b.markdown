## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an uncompromising, high-contrast editorial identity rooted in Swiss grid discipline and fine literary publishing. Engineered for a personal portfolio, the aesthetic projects intellectual rigor, technical exactness, and timeless typographic sophistication. 

The emotional impact is architectural, authoritative, and stripped of ornamental distraction. Depth is communicated strictly through hairline rules, typographic scale, and structural framing. Tactile character is reinforced using subtle, fine-grained analog paper noise textures, while spatial structure is demarcated by explicit 1px monochrome gridlines. Every boundary is absolute: sharp corners, zero blur, and instantaneous or precise linear transitions.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict binary monochrome. Color exists solely in tones of absolute black, archival whites, and functional structural greys.

- **Primary (`#000000`):** Pure optical black. Applied to dominant titles, heavy 2px/4px/8px structural rules, solid button fills, and primary high-contrast borders.
- **Secondary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep carbon black. Utilized for continuous long-form body copy to soften stark optical vibration against pristine backgrounds while maintaining AAA accessibility.
- **Tertiary (`#767676`):** Mid-tone neutral grey. Restricted to technical metadata, publication datelines, index tags, and secondary indicators.
- **Neutral (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure unbleached white. The baseline paper canvas and inverse text fill for solid black modules.

Functional borders utilize `#000000` for deliberate architectural boundaries and `#E5E5E5` for subtle, recurring hairline grid frameworks. Interactive states rely strictly on direct color inversion: black inverted to white, white to black. No chromatic accents are permitted.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical high-contrast serif headlines with a bookmaker’s body text, anchored by a rigid monospaced engine for metadata and technical annotations.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys literary stature and high-editorial drama. Used for major project titles, chapter dividers, and section headings. Keep tracking tight on large sizes to preserve editorial density.
- **Body Text (Source Serif 4):** Ensures long-form reading comfort, editorial rhythm, and humanist warmth. Optimized for design essays, case study narratives, and project briefs.
- **Labels & System Meta (JetBrains Mono):** Set exclusively in monospaced uppercase or strict numeric alignments for dates, tags, figure captions, table outputs, and system telemetry. All mono labels above `0.75rem` require an increased letter-spacing to enhance legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an explicit 12-column columnar grid defined by visible or implied structural lines. 

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12 columns with a fixed `margin` of `3rem` and `gutter` of `2rem`. Sections are partitioned by solid continuous horizontal rules. Maximum content container width is capped at `1440px` for optimal typographic line-length.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Project galleries collapse from 3 columns to 2.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem`, and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Structural borders switch to full bleed, snapping edge-to-edge.

Spacing rhythm follows an incremental mathematical ratio (`0.25rem` baseline). Micro-spacing within interactive components remains tightly constrained, while section boundaries utilize generous structural voids (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) to evoke an open broadsheet page.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects drop shadows, z-axis lighting, blurred skeuomorphism, and simulated depth. The canvas is entirely planar. 

Visual hierarchy and spatial separation are established through typographic hierarchy, massing, and a strict system of linework:
- **Hairline Rules (1px solid #000000 or #E5E5E5):** Used for grid boundaries, metadata enclosures, list separators, and card containers.
- **Structural Dividers (2px solid #000000):** Used for section separators, active tab bars, and secondary headers.
- **Major Accent Bars (4px solid #000000):** Used for primary section dividers and critical callout margins.
- **Monumental Anchors (8px solid #000000):** Used for the masthead terminal rule, footer anchors, or prominent hero accents.
- **Paper Noise:** A persistent, low-opacity (2–3%) monochromatic SVG noise texture is applied across the background to emulate uncoated physical paper stock.
- **CSS Line Grids:** Delicate background repeating gridlines (`1px` lines spaced at `2rem` intervals in `#F0F0F0`) provide subtle underlying structure to full-bleed hero and gallery panels.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly rectilinear. All UI primitives—including buttons, input fields, badges, cards, modal sheets, and image masks—utilize an immutable border radius of `0px`. 

Corners are sharp right angles, honoring industrial print traditions and geometric minimalism. Geometric variety is achieved strictly through aspect ratio transformations, border stroke weights, and negative space.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid black fill (`#000000`), white label (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #000000`, `0px` radius. Font: `JetBrains Mono`, uppercase, tracked out. On hover: invert immediately to solid white fill with black text.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #000000`, black label. On hover: invert to solid black fill with white text.
- **Ghost:** Text-only in `JetBrains Mono` with a persistent `1px` underline situated `4px` beneath text baseline. On hover: underline expands to `2px`.

### Chips & Badges
- Encased in a `1px solid #000000` rectangular box with `0px` radius.
- Padding: `0.25rem` top/bottom, `0.5rem` left/right.
- Typography: `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm`, uppercase. Background is `#FFFFFF` by default; toggled or selected states invert to `#000000` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Lists
- Tabular and editorial presentation. Each list row is delimited by a top or bottom `1px solid #000000` rule (or `#E5E5E5` for secondary streams).
